令和８年熊本地震に対し、多くの国の首脳を含め、世界中の皆様から温かいお見舞いと連帯のお言葉を頂いており、心から感謝申し上げます。 皆様からの励ましにより、日本国民は大変大きな勇気を頂いております。 また、これまでに多数の国や国際機関などからの支援の申し出を頂いており、深く感謝申し上げます。 現在、被災した地域において人命救助や被災地支援に日本国政府として全力を挙げて取り組んでおります。 今後の現地の状況やニーズを見極めながら、どのような形でお力添えをお願いできるか考えてまいります。 皆様からのお見舞いと支援の申し出に、改めて感謝申し上げます。 I would like to extend my sincere gratitude for the warm messages of sympathy and solidarity we have received from people around the world, including the leaders of a number of countries, following the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake. The encouragement from all of you has been a tremendous source of courage for the people of Japan. I would also like to express my heartfelt appreciation for the offers of support we have received from numerous countries and international organizations so far. The Government of Japan is working in earnest to carry out rescue operations and provide assistance to the affected areas. As we continue to assess the situation and needs on the ground, we will carefully consider how the generous offers of support can best be utilized. Once again, I would like to express my deep gratitude for your kind messages of support and offers of assistance. @GiorgiaMeloni @LulaOficial @predsednikrs @NicusorDanRO @Jaemyung_Lee @EmmanuelMacron
